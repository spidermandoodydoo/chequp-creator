# Creative direction: Vicky × Sophie, 7–8 Oct 2026

Source: the Vicky / Sophie / Dan group DM (7 Oct 16:51 to 8 Oct 09:01), plus Sophie's doc "Campaign idea" (Google Doc 18tG6K7q…).

## What was decided

1. **Pivot from brand to direct response.** Vicky: "the creative no longer feels right… radio isn't cutting through and VOD isn't yet either."
   - Reddit is already paused.
   - VOD is to be paused while "alternative / harder-hitting creative" is made.
   - Context: VOD drove 114 tagged visits and **0 sales** from 28 Sep to 7 Oct. The money is Toby's, and he asked for "brave choices".
2. **Platform line: "Weight loss made simple with The CheqUp Method."** It was the strongest headline in Vypr testing, and Vicky likes it.
3. **Don't lead with "Simple takes a lot of work. Most of it you'll never see."** It risks sounding like the competitor **Simple Online Pharmacy**. So "simple" goes in the platform line, never as a standalone opener or campaign name.
4. **Less subtle, braver, not understated**, even for brand.
5. **Face the customer problem head-on.** The reference is Numan's "Cardboard Doctors" (Execute, Aug 2026): "weight loss treatment takes more than a prescription", calling out cardboard-cut-out care. Don't copy it, but be as clear as it is.
6. **Benchmark: last year's DR-focused VOD by YeahNice**, with the CheqUp Method surfaced much more clearly. Sophie or Karen may know where the file is; it isn't in the Meta library.

## Sophie's idea, and how Vicky's notes change it

**Sophie's "The work behind simple":** an ordinary moment that has gone right (a packed lunch, trainers by the door, a calm food shop). An understated line then reveals the work behind it:
- "Planned on Thursday by her Health Coach, around her night shifts"
- "Her health reviewed by a UK Clinician who knows her history"

The look is editorial photography, natural light, small precise type and sparse music.

**What Vicky's notes change:**

| Keep | Change |
|---|---|
| Moments that went right, each showing one part of the Method | "Small precise type": make the reveal **big and direct**. The line is the hero, not a caption |
| Clinician, Health Coach and WeightWatchers app, shown concretely | Open on the **problem** before the moment (the Numan lesson) |
| Real homes, natural light. This is the design system's photography rule | End on a **DR close**: platform line, "Check my eligibility", URL |
| Platform line "Weight loss made simple with The CheqUp Method" | Don't open with the "Simple takes…" campaign line |

## The customer problem, from CheqUp's own data

- **"Medication without support"** is the title of one of CheqUp's own explainers. It's the CheqUp version of Cardboard Doctors: providers who stop at the order.
- **The yo-yo:** "Spent years losing weight, then gaining it back?" is a live hook, and the LTN ad used "dieting roller coaster".
- **Doing it alone.** The Coach angle converts: SUPPORT B – GOALS ran at £129–183 CPA.
- **Comparing providers** is another of their explainer titles. People can't tell who is real.

## Compliance guardrails (they shape how hard it can hit)

- **Name the problem as a service problem:** "a website and a delivery", "no one to talk to". Never "a prescription", "a jab" or "an injection". CheqUp has upheld ASA rulings, and Numan names the medicine in ways CheqUp can't.
- **No disparaging a named competitor.** Calling out a category behaviour, Numan-style, is fine.
- **Don't open with "simple" as a standalone word.** Keep it inside the platform line.
- **"Planned by her Coach, around her night shifts" is illustrative.** Label it, or use only details from consenting members.
